# Contributor Covenant — CommunityConnect

## Our pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards
Positive behavior: disagreeing respectfully, owning mistakes, seeking diverse perspectives, showing empathy.
Unacceptable: sexualized language or attention, trolling/insulting/derogatory comments, harassment (public or private), publishing others’ private information without consent, or other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities
Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair, appropriate action for unacceptable behavior, explaining reasons when feasible. The in-product moderation console records outcomes with reasons; appeals are supported.

## Scope
This document applies within all project spaces (repo, issues, chat) and when an individual officially represents the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer via GitHub (@siddhant-pardeshi) or the in-app report flow. All complaints are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Reporter identity is kept confidential to the extent possible.
