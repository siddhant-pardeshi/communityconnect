# Brand — names, taglines & voice

> “CommunityConnect” is a working name. Nothing here is trademark- or domain-cleared — run clearance before a branded launch.

## Name candidates (ranked)

| # | Name | Why it sticks | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Orbitly** | Matches the constellation/orbit brand art perfectly; “orbit” = your people + your event circle; sounds like a product, not a feature | Check .com/.in availability; easy to spell aloud |
| 2 | **CrewUp** | Verb-able (“let's CrewUp this weekend”); crew = exactly who the product serves | Many small squatters; needs clearance |
| 3 | **Huddl** | A huddle is a fast group decision — the Decision Room in one word; short, typo-resistant (get huddl.com/.app variants) | Pronunciation is obvious but trademark space is crowded |
| 4 | **Saathi** (साथी) | Deep emotional resonance in India — “companion”; perfect for the buddy + support-hub story | Widely used name (govt portals, NGOs); hard to own; consider “SaathiCircle” |
| 5 | **Junto** | “Together” in Spanish; historically Benjamin Franklin's civic circle — great founder story | Less obvious to Indian audiences; say-it-out-loud test needed |

**Recommendation:** keep **CommunityConnect** as the company/project name (it describes the mission), and shortlist **Orbitly** (global) or **Saathi** (India-first) as the consumer product name after clearance. The codebase and domain references do not need to change on rename — the brand lives in one config place (`index.html` title + manifest + README).

## Taglines (primary + rotations)

**Primary (in product today):**
> **Find your event. Bring your people.**

**Sticky rotations (test in launch copy):**
1. **Never event alone.** — the most memorable; leads the buddy story
2. **From three links to one plan.** — explains the Decision Room instantly
3. **Decide together. Show up together.** — the promise, symmetric
4. **Your crew has chosen.** — warm, confident, already in the product UI
5. **Turn “who's coming?” into “see you there.”** — names the pain
6. **Plans are better with your people in them.**
7. **Akela nahi. Poore crew ke saath.** — Hinglish campaign line for campus launches

**Anti-recommendations:** avoid anything with “network” (LinkedIn shadow), “meetup” (trademark), or generic AI-era words (“superapp”, “AI-powered”).

## Voice rules (used across the product)
- Warm, brief, specific. “Your circle has chosen. Confirm whether you can attend.” — not “Action required.”
- Never loss-framed: no countdown guilt, no public shame, no “don't miss out”.
- Availability is never a commitment; a plan is never a ticket; a vouch is never a verdict.
- Honesty is the brand: label demo data, show the scoring formula, admit unknown prices.
